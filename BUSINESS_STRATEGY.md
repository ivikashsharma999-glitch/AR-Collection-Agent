# AR Collections Agent: Business & Pricing Strategy

Based on the competitive landscape (HighRadius, Tesorio, Upflow) and standard B2B SaaS pricing models for AI FinTech tools, this agent represents a highly monetizable asset. You are entering a market where companies are currently paying massive premiums for software that is entirely rules-based and lacks real AI reasoning.

## The Market Context (What Competitors Charge)

*   **Enterprise Legacy (HighRadius, Billtrust):** Charge **$50,000 to $100,000+ per year** plus massive implementation fees (3-6 months setup). They are slow, rigid, and lack the AI reasoning you have built.
*   **Mid-Market (Tesorio, Upflow):** Typically charge **$1,000 to $3,000+ per month** based on the volume of revenue processed or number of active invoices.
*   **SMB / Basic Reminders (Chaser, Invoiced):** Charge **$100 to $500 per month**. These are mostly "dumb" email sequencers without AI diagnosis or compliance filters.

---

## Your Value Proposition

Because your agent acts autonomously, diagnoses *why* a customer isn't paying, and adapts its tone dynamically, you are no longer just selling "software"—you are selling a **digital employee** (an AI AR Clerk). 

A human AR clerk costs a company $50,000–$70,000/year ($4k–$6k/month). You can easily justify premium pricing because you are offering the output of a human clerk at a fraction of the cost, operating 24/7.

Your biggest selling points:
1. **Compliance Shield & Safety:** By showing them the dashboard where the AI explicitly explains *why* it chose a specific tone and *blocks* aggressive language, you instantly overcome their biggest objection to AI automation.
2. **Hard ROI:** You are selling a tool that literally puts cash in their bank account. If an agency has $50,000 sitting in overdue invoices, and your tool recovers $30,000 of it in the first month, they will happily pay you for the service.

---

## Recommended Pricing Tiers for Launch

### 1. The "Pilot" / Growth Tier (SMBs & Startups)
*   **Price:** $499 - $899 / month 
*   **Target:** B2B SaaS companies processing $1M–$10M in ARR.
*   **Value Prop:** Cheaper than hiring a part-time collections assistant. Completely automates the tedious follow-up work while maintaining brand reputation through the Adaptive Tone Engine.

### 2. The "Pro" / Mid-Market Tier
*   **Price:** $1,500 - $3,000 / month
*   **Target:** Companies with $10M–$50M ARR processing thousands of invoices.
*   **Value Prop:** This is where your **Non-Payment Diagnosis** and **Compliance Shield** shine. At this scale, manual tracking fails. Companies will gladly pay $2k/mo to accelerate cash flow and reduce Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) by 10-15 days.

### 3. Value-Based "Success Fee" Model (Alternative)
*   Instead of a flat SaaS fee, you can charge a low base fee (e.g., $299/mo) + **0.5% to 1% of all past-due revenue successfully recovered** by the agent. If the AI recovers a $50k invoice that was 60 days late, you take $500. This makes it a complete no-brainer for companies to say "yes" to your pilot.

## Realistic Income Projections

You can sell this software as a **Multi-Tenant SaaS** (many companies logging into your platform) or operate as an **"AI-Powered AR Agency"** (you run the software on their behalf).

### The "Easy" Realistic Path (First 6-12 Months)
Target local small-to-medium B2B businesses (agencies, SaaS companies, wholesalers, consultancies) that don't have dedicated finance teams.

*   **Your Price:** **$300 / month** (An absolute steal compared to a $20k/year human clerk).
*   **Your Goal:** Get just **10 clients**.

> **10 Clients × $300/mo = $3,000 Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)**
> *(Or $36,000 per year of mostly passive income).*

### The "Scaling" Realistic Path (Year 1 to 2)
Once the software proves it works (the AI doesn't make mistakes, and companies see their cash flow improve), you can raise your prices for new clients and move slightly upmarket to companies doing $5M–$20M in revenue.

*   **Your Price:** **$750 / month**
*   **Your Goal:** **25 clients**

> **25 Clients × $750/mo = $18,750 Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)**
> *(Or $225,000 per year).*

## Your Immediate Action Plan

1. **Get 3 Free "Beta" Testers:** Find 3 businesses (friends, or local companies) and offer it to them for **free** for 60 days. Tell them, *"I built an AI that chases overdue invoices gently and professionally. I'll run it for you for free just to get your feedback."*
2. **Collect Case Studies:** Document how much money the AI recovered for those 3 businesses. (e.g., *"Recovered $42,000 in late payments in 14 days without human intervention"*).
3. **Start Charging:** Use those case studies to sell to the 4th business for $300/month.
