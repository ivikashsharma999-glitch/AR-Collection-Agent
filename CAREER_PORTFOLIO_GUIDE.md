# AR Collections Agent: Resume & Portfolio Guide

Building this complex, AI-native application from scratch puts you in an elite category of **AI Product Builders**. It demonstrates that you understand how to apply AI to solve real-world business problems (like cash flow and late payments) safely and securely.

Here is exactly how to leverage this project to land high-paying roles or pitch yourself to investors.

---

## 📄 How to Write it on Your Resume

*Copy and paste this directly into your "Experience" or "Projects" section:*

**Role:** Founder / AI Product Architect
**Project:** Autonomous AR Collections Agent
*   Architected and built a multi-tenant, AI-native B2B SaaS application using Next.js and Supabase designed to automate Accounts Receivable (AR) collections and reduce Days Sales Outstanding (DSO).
*   Designed an **LLM-powered Non-Payment Diagnosis Engine** that analyzes email reply history to autonomously categorize non-payment reasons (e.g., cash flow, active disputes) and adapt follow-up tone dynamically.
*   Engineered a hard-coded **Compliance Shield** to mitigate AI hallucination risks, using strict filtering and rate-limiting to prevent the AI from generating aggressive language or violating strict communication caps.
*   Integrated third-party OAuth flows (QuickBooks, Google Workspace, Microsoft Graph) to simulate end-to-end data synchronization between accounting ledgers and email outboxes.

## 🌐 How to Showcase it on a Portfolio Website

Don't just post a GitHub link. Non-technical recruiters, hiring managers, and CEOs won't read code. Your portfolio needs to tell a visual story. Include the following 3 sections on your personal website:

### 1. The "Hero" Video (Crucial)
Record a 2-minute Loom video of you walking through the dashboard. 
*   Show the beautiful onboarding flow.
*   Show the premium UI and dashboard analytics.
*   **Highlight the "AI Reasoning" panel** where the AI explicitly explains *why* it chose a certain tone. This proves it's not just a blind automated emailer.

### 2. The Architecture Diagram
Draw a simple, professional diagram showing how the pieces connect. This proves you understand systems engineering and API orchestration:
`QuickBooks (Ledger Data)` ➡️ `Supabase (Secure Storage)` ➡️ `OpenAI (Reasoning Engine)` ➡️ `Gmail (Execution)`

### 3. The "Why I Built It" Section
Explain the business problem and the ROI. 
*   *The Problem:* Late payments kill MSMEs, and existing tools just send dumb, generic reminders that get ignored.
*   *The Solution:* An intelligent agent that reads replies, diagnoses the blockage, and acts like a highly-trained, empathetic finance clerk.

## 🎯 What Job Titles Should You Apply For?

With this project on your resume, you are highly qualified for roles that bridge the gap between business strategy and technology. You should target:

1.  **AI Product Manager:** Guiding engineering teams on what AI features to build next based on market needs.
2.  **Technical Product Manager (TPM):** Managing complex integrations (like OAuth and webhooks) and architecture decisions.
3.  **AI Solutions Architect / Consultant:** Helping traditional, non-tech companies figure out how to safely implement AI into their daily workflows.
4.  **Founding Team / Early Startup Employee:** Y-Combinator startups love "generalists" who can design, prompt, and build full systems end-to-end without needing hand-holding.
